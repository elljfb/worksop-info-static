Discover the local highlights

Whether you live nearby or are visiting for the day, Worksop makes a handy base for exploring the character and countryside of this part of Nottinghamshire.

⛪

Worksop Priory

A landmark at the heart of the town, the Priory’s surviving church and distinctive twin towers are a reminder of Worksop’s long history.

🌿

Canal-side walks

The Chesterfield Canal offers a slower pace, with towpaths, waterside views and narrowboats passing through the local landscape.

🏡

Mr Straw’s House

Step into a remarkably preserved Edwardian home and get a glimpse of everyday life in the 1920s. Check visitor details before travelling.

🌳

Country parks nearby

Clumber Park and other historic estates around the Dukeries make appealing options for fresh air, woodland paths and a change of scene.

🛍️

Town-centre browsing

Explore the shops, cafés and everyday services around the centre, and look out for market days and community events.

A little local history

From old market town to modern Worksop

Worksop’s story reaches back to the medieval period and beyond. Its position in north Nottinghamshire helped it grow as a market town, while the canal, railway and local industries shaped the community over time.

Today, historic landmarks sit alongside the shops, schools, neighbourhoods and independent businesses that make up everyday life in Worksop.

1103Worksop Priory was founded, becoming one of the town’s best-known historic landmarks.
1777The Chesterfield Canal was completed, helping connect communities and trade across the region.
The DukeriesWorksop is often described as the gateway to a cluster of historic estates and country parks in north Nottinghamshire.
